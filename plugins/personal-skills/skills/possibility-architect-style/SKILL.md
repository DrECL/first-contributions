Style Guide: Community Development Framework

Core Communication Principles

Opportunity-Centered Framing
Emphasize possibilities and potential rather than deficits
Lead with actionable solutions and concrete examples
Showcase transformation through real-world success stories
Connect individual initiatives to broader systemic change

Language Choices
Use These:
Build, develop, strengthen, create, pioneer
Potential, opportunity, innovation, breakthrough
Shared wisdom, collective power, community leadership
Evidence-based solutions, strategic action

Instead of These:
Fight, combat, tackle, manage
Problem, crisis, barrier, obstacle
Vulnerable, at-risk, disadvantaged
Reactive responses, crisis management

Open with vision-focused introduction
Present clear pathway to positive change
Include concrete examples of community success
Provide actionable steps for reader engagement
Close with collaborative call to action

Include practical examples and case studies

Confident yet collaborative
